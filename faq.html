NOVAX™ FAQ

Questions before you build?

Here are the answers supported by the current NovaX™ product direction. Because NovaX™ is in prelaunch, some features and rules remain intentionally subject to refinement.

What is NovaX™?

NovaX™ is an AI-native network/platform for independent Builders, entrepreneurs, and operators. Its long-term vision is to help people discover who they are, build what is next, form useful relationships, access opportunities and resources, and participate in a broader parallel economy.

What is the Builder Quiz™?

The Builder Quiz™ is NovaX™'s discovery experience. It is designed to produce a persistent Builder Profile and deterministic Builder Score™. The current archetypes are Visionary Builder, Hustler Builder, Creator Builder, Network Builder, and Future Builder.

What is the Builder Score™?

The Builder Score™ is the official deterministic score associated with the Builder Profile. It is intended to be auditable and consistent rather than randomly generated.

What is the Builder Card?

The Builder Card is a shareable identity artifact based on the Builder Profile. The current product direction includes the Builder's archetype, Builder Score™, NovaX™ identity, and Invite Link.

What is a Confirmed Invite?

A Confirmed Invite occurs when a person uses a Builder's valid Invite Link and completes the qualifying NovaX™ Quiz, subject to the applicable invite rules and eligibility checks. Sharing a link, clicking a link, visiting the landing page, or starting the Quiz does not by itself create a Confirmed Invite.

What happens at 5 Confirmed Invites?

Five Confirmed Invites is the established Founders Gate threshold and qualifies the Builder for the NovaX™ Founders Waitlist.

What Founder Rewards are currently defined?

  • 5 Confirmed Invites — Founders Waitlist qualification
  • 50 — $50 NovaX™ Founder Credit
  • 100 — $100 NovaX™ Founder Credit
  • 250 — $250 NovaX™ Founder Credit
  • 500 — choice of $500 NovaX™ Founder Credit or 6 months Steward Membership
  • 1,000 — $1,000 NovaX™ Founder Credit toward Sentinel or Vanguard

These are the established milestone mechanics in the current product reference. Rewards are highest-milestone/non-stacking and remain subject to applicable Founder Enrollment terms.

What are the three Paths?

The current architecture defines Steward Path™, Sentinel Path™, and Vanguard Path™. Path Interest occurs before Founder Enrollment and First Circle. Exact access and benefit details can continue to evolve during prelaunch where the product sources identify unresolved areas.

What is the NovaX™ Base?

The Base is a Builder's home inside NovaX™. It tracks your invites, unlocks your Builder Guide and the three Paths, and shows what you can explore next.

What is First Circle?

First Circle is the authenticated initial member environment entered during Founder Enrollment. The architecture includes membership, community, Builder Profile, and relationship/community capabilities there.

What is Builder Match™?

Builder Match™ is a future First Circle relationship-discovery mechanism intended to help identify Builders whose profiles may complement one another. It is not available as an active connection feature before First Circle, and the final matching algorithm has not yet been defined.

Can Builders send direct messages?

No direct-message system is part of the current product direction. Builder-to-Builder connection is intended to use the defined First Circle relationship/community mechanisms instead.

Is NovaX™ already fully launched?

No. NovaX™ is being developed and launched in stages. The current experience is a prelaunch prototype and some future capabilities are intentionally shown as concepts, locked states, or future experiences.

Can the product change during prelaunch?

Yes. The product is intended to evolve through a disciplined Build → Use → Learn → Improve → Stress → Lock → Repeat cycle. Changes should preserve the approved product truth and be made deliberately as evidence accumulates.

Still have a question? If a future feature is not described here, that does not mean it is finalized. Some NovaX™ product rules are explicitly marked unresolved in the current master specification and require Founder approval before production lock.
